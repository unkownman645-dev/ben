Clinical Safety & Compliance Note: This is a functional prototype. Before clinical deployment with real patient health information (PHI), production implementations require HIPAA-compliant encrypted storage at rest and in transit, Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with API providers, a dedicated human-in-the-loop pager escalation system for high-acuity triggers, and clinical oversight/sign-off on question protocols.
